# घर की अदालत (Ghar Ki Adalat — The Family Court) — Standalone Episode

- **Series:** semi-realistic animated Hindi joint-family channel (standalone, fc01)
- **Runtime target:** 10 min of VOICE (131 Hindi words/min, measured on Ep02)
- **Measured dialogue word count:** 1308 Hindi dialogue words (tags, actions and cards excluded) = 10.0 min at 131 wpm; to be re-measured after recording
- **Written:** 10 Oct 2026 (owner-approved plot; script awaiting owner approval, no audio yet)
- **Logline:** Kamla's thirty-year-old pickle jar has vanished and she blames her bahu Sunita. Nine-year-old Chinki, sick of the shouting, turns the living room into a courtroom — steel plate for a gavel, Dadaji's specs, the sofa for a witness box. Every witness tells it differently, until Golu's toy "CCTV" points at Ammaji's walking stick, and Ammaji confesses that she broke the ORIGINAL jar sixty years ago and secretly bought this one so her own saas wouldn't scold her. Verdict: Kamla and Sunita are both guilty of one crime — never asking each other "why".
- **Tone:** courtroom comedy with real heart. Nobody is a villain. Sunita's body is never a joke.
- **Cast (existing designs only):** CHINKI (9, judge), KAMLA (saas), SUNITA (badi bahu), AMMAJI (Kamla's saas, ~80), GOLU (7, toy CCTV), MAHESH (Kamla's son, Sunita's husband), NEHA (nanad, live-streamer). Dadaji (RAMPRASAD) is at the temple; only his specs appear.
- **Ages check:** Kamla has lived in this house about forty years; Ammaji handed her the kitchen and the jar THIRTY years ago (ten years after she came). Ammaji broke the original jar SIXTY years ago as a new bahu.
- **Camera rules:** locked camera. In the living-room court EVERYONE present stays in frame (COURT framing, see MOTION_PLAN.md). Prop inserts are hands-only CLOSE shots with no dialogue. Slow push-in only on the sepia flashbacks. Flashback shots are marked `(FLASHBACK)` in the shot header; voice-over is marked in ACTION, and the narrator is always visible inside their own flashback.
- **Parser notes:** speaker tags are CHINKI, KAMLA, SUNITA, AMMAJI, GOLU, MAHESH, NEHA. Emotion in brackets is always an exact key from `episodes/emotion_bible.json`. Inline tags only `<laugh> <sigh> <gasp> <short pause>`, and only where that emotion allows them. `TITLE:` and `CARD:` lines are ENGLISH on-screen text in the lower band (rulebook §4), not dialogue. `[SHORT]` marks a Shorts-worthy shot.
