# The Heart-Talk Radio (Dil Ki Baat Radio) — Kulfi Kahani Episode 2
  _हिंदी: दिल की बात रेडियो_

> English review copy of `script_hi.md`. Only the Hindi dialogue and cards are translated; the original Hindi sits under each line in italics.

- Runtime: ~9 min · Made for kids · Style: Kulfi cartoon (flat 2D)
- Logline: Jugaadu Chacha builds a radio that broadcasts what people really feel. At the Diwali mela the village's secret thoughts blare out — funny, until the radio plays Dadi's loneliness and the children realise they never sit with her anymore.
- Moral: Spend time with your elders — they need us just as much as we need them.
  _हिंदी: बड़ों के साथ समय बिताओ — उन्हें हमारी उतनी ही ज़रूरत है जितनी हमें उनकी।_
- Motion recipes: docs/MOTION_GUIDE.md (SWAP big poses, BEND small life, PROC legs; on twos; locked camera).

## Scene 1 — Village square at the Diwali mela — Evening
BG: A village square decorated for Diwali at dusk — strings of marigold and small electric lights, sweet stalls, a balloon seller, diyas on a low wall, a big old loudspeaker tied to a neem tree.

### Shot 1.1 — WIDE — Lallan, villagers
ACTION: Lallan stands behind his sweet stall, smiling at customers; the loudspeaker on the tree crackles.
LOUDSPEAKER (booming, comic): "Lallan's sweets — half sugar, half sneaky tricks!"
  _हिंदी: "लल्लन की मिठाई में आधी चीनी, आधी चालाकी!"_
LALLAN (shocked): Hey! Who said that?
  _हिंदी: अरे! ये किसने बोला?_

### Shot 1.2 — CLOSE — Lallan
ACTION: Lallan freezes, a laddoo in mid-air; surprised take.
LALLAN (panicked): But I… I was only thinking it in my head!
  _हिंदी: मैं तो… मैं तो बस मन में सोच रहा था!_
[SHORT]

## Scene 2 — Jugaadu Chacha's workshop — Morning (same day)
BG: A cluttered village workshop under a tin roof — shelves of old radios, bicycle wheels, a pressure cooker with wires, a blackboard with drawings, morning sun through the door.

### Shot 2.1 — WIDE — Jugaadu Chacha, Chhotu, Gudiya
ACTION: Chhotu and Gudiya walk in; Chacha (white coat, goggles) bends over an old transistor radio with a stethoscope wired to it.
TITLE CARD: Morning — before the mela
  _हिंदी: सुबह — मेले से पहले_
CHHOTU (excited): Chacha! What machine are you making today?
  _हिंदी: चाचा! आज कौन-सी मशीन बना रहे हो?_
JUGAADU CHACHA (proud): Behold… the "Heart-Talk Radio"! Point it at anyone, and you'll hear what their heart is saying.
  _हिंदी: ये है… "दिल की बात रेडियो"! जिसकी तरफ़ घुमाओ, उसके दिल की आवाज़ सुनाई देगी।_

### Shot 2.2 — MEDIUM — Gudiya, Chhotu
ACTION: Gudiya points at the radio; Chhotu explains with his hands.
GUDIYA (doubtful): What the heart says? Chacha, a heart just goes thump-thump, it doesn't talk!
  _हिंदी: दिल की आवाज़? चाचा, दिल तो धक-धक करता है, बात थोड़ी करता है!_
JUGAADU CHACHA (mysterious): Oh, it talks, little one. It's just that nobody listens.
  _हिंदी: करता है, बिटिया। बस कोई सुनता नहीं।_

### Shot 2.3 — MEDIUM — Chacha, Sheru
ACTION: Chacha turns the radio towards Sheru, who sits wagging his tail.
JUGAADU CHACHA (excited): Watch! First test — Sheru!
  _हिंदी: देखो! पहला टेस्ट — शेरू!_
RADIO (Sheru's heart, cheeky): I want a laddoo too! Not one — TWO!
  _हिंदी: मुझे भी लड्डू चाहिए! एक नहीं, दो!_
CHHOTU (laughing): Hahaha! Chacha, it actually works!
  _हिंदी: हाहाहा! चाचा, ये तो सच में काम करता है!_
[SHORT]

### Shot 2.4 — CLOSE — Jugaadu Chacha
ACTION: Chacha raises a warning finger.
JUGAADU CHACHA (serious): But remember — it isn't ready yet. Nobody takes it outside!
  _हिंदी: पर ध्यान रहे, अभी ये पूरी तरह तैयार नहीं है। इसे कोई बाहर नहीं ले जाएगा!_
CHHOTU (innocent): Of course not, Chacha!
  _हिंदी: बिल्कुल नहीं, चाचा!_

### Shot 2.5 — MEDIUM — Chhotu, Gudiya
ACTION: Chacha turns his back; Chhotu and Gudiya exchange a look, grab the radio and sneak out of the door.
GUDIYA (whisper): Just for a little while…
  _हिंदी: बस थोड़ी देर के लिए…_
CHHOTU (whisper): It'll be SO much fun at the mela!
  _हिंदी: मेले में बड़ा मज़ा आएगा!_

## Scene 3 — The Diwali mela — Evening
BG: The same Diwali mela, closer: a jalebi stall with a big kadhai, a toy stall, a temple with steps and a bell at one side, children with balloons.

### Shot 3.1 — WIDE — Chhotu, Gudiya, Raju
ACTION: The kids walk in through the crowd; Raju runs up with a kite.
RAJU (boastful): Look at my kite! It'll fly the highest in the whole village!
  _हिंदी: देखो मेरी पतंग! पूरे गाँव में सबसे ऊँची उड़ेगी!_
CHHOTU (mischief): Oh really? Radio, let's hear what Raju's heart has to say!
  _हिंदी: अच्छा? रेडियो, ज़रा राजू के दिल की सुनाओ!_
RADIO (Raju's heart, sheepish): Gudiya flies a kite better than me… but I will NEVER admit it!
  _हिंदी: गुड़िया मुझसे अच्छी पतंग उड़ाती है… पर ये मैं कभी नहीं मानूँगा!_
GUDIYA (proud): Yes! Did everyone hear that?
  _हिंदी: हाँ! सुना सबने?_
RAJU (embarrassed): Hey! That… that machine is a liar!
  _हिंदी: अरे! ये… ये तो झूठी मशीन है!_

### Shot 3.2 — MEDIUM — Masterji
ACTION: Masterji walks past, folding his hands to greet the villagers, very dignified.
MASTERJI (formal): Children, be on time for school tomorrow. Discipline is very important.
  _हिंदी: बच्चो, कल स्कूल समय पर आना। अनुशासन बहुत ज़रूरी है।_
RADIO (Masterji's heart, dreamy): If only tomorrow were a holiday too… and I could sleep all day.
  _हिंदी: काश कल भी छुट्टी होती… और मैं दिन भर सोता रहता।_
MASTERJI (stunned): Wh… whose voice was that?
  _हिंदी: ये… ये किसकी आवाज़ है?_

### Shot 3.3 — WIDE — Lallan, villagers, kids
ACTION: Lallan points at the kids; villagers turn; the kids hide the radio behind their backs.
LALLAN (angry): There they are! They've got some magic box in their hands!
  _हिंदी: वो रहे! उनके हाथ में कोई जादुई डिब्बा है!_
GUDIYA (scared): Run!
  _हिंदी: भागो!_

### Shot 3.4 — WIDE — kids running, Lallan, Masterji, Raju chasing
ACTION: A comic chase through the mela — the kids run between stalls, Lallan runs holding his dhoti, Masterji runs holding his glasses, Raju runs with his kite. Jugaadu Chacha arrives running from the other side.
JUGAADU CHACHA (shouting): Stop! It's not ready yet!
  _हिंदी: रुको! वो अभी तैयार नहीं है!_
[SHORT]

## Scene 4 — Behind the temple, near Dadi's house — Night
BG: The quiet back lane behind the village temple at night — a low mud wall, a single diya, and across the lane Dadi's courtyard with a charpai under a lantern; the mela lights glow far away.

### Shot 4.1 — MEDIUM — Chhotu, Gudiya
ACTION: The kids sneak in and crouch behind the wall, out of breath.
CHHOTU (out of breath): We made it!
  _हिंदी: बच गए!_
GUDIYA (giggling): Did you see Lallan Chacha's face?
  _हिंदी: लल्लन चाचा का चेहरा देखा था?_

### Shot 4.2 — CLOSE — the radio
ACTION: The radio slips from Chhotu's hands, rolls and stops pointing across the lane at Dadi's courtyard.

### Shot 4.3 — WIDE — Dadi alone
ACTION: Dadi sits alone on her charpai under the lantern, a plate of laddoos beside her, looking at the far-away mela lights.
RADIO (Dadi's heart, soft and sad): They've all grown up… nobody comes to hear my stories anymore.
  _हिंदी: सब बड़े हो गए… अब कोई कहानी सुनने नहीं आता।_
RADIO (Dadi's heart, softer): I made laddoos for the children again today… but no one came.
  _हिंदी: आज भी बच्चों के लिए लड्डू बनाए थे… पर कोई आया ही नहीं।_

### Shot 4.4 — CLOSE — Chhotu, Gudiya
ACTION: The kids stop laughing; Gudiya's eyes fill with tears; Chhotu lowers his head.
GUDIYA (quiet, sad): Dadi… is sitting all alone.
  _हिंदी: दादी… अकेली बैठी हैं।_
CHHOTU (guilty): Dadi called me this morning… and I said, "Later, Dadi."
  _हिंदी: सुबह दादी ने बुलाया था… मैंने कहा, "बाद में, दादी।"_

### Shot 4.5 — WIDE — Lallan, Masterji, Raju arrive and stop
ACTION: The chasers arrive and stop; they have heard it too; everyone stands still.
LALLAN (soft, ashamed): We were all at the mela… and Dadi was here, alone.
  _हिंदी: हम सब मेले में थे… और दादी यहाँ अकेली।_
MASTERJI (moved): The radio spoke the truth.
  _हिंदी: सच ही कहा था रेडियो ने।_

### Shot 4.6 — MEDIUM — Jugaadu Chacha
ACTION: Chacha walks in slowly, picks up the radio and switches it off.
JUGAADU CHACHA (gentle): Some things you don't hear on a radio… you hear them by sitting close.
  _हिंदी: कुछ बातें रेडियो से नहीं… पास बैठने से सुनाई देती हैं।_

## Scene 5 — Dadi's courtyard — Night
BG: Dadi's courtyard at night — a charpai under a lantern, a tulsi plant, rows of lit diyas being placed on the low wall, the mela lights far away.

### Shot 5.1 — WIDE — kids, Dadi
ACTION: The kids walk in carrying diyas, a plate of jalebi and Sheru wearing a paper crown; Dadi looks up surprised.
CHHOTU (bright): Dadi! We brought the mela to you!
  _हिंदी: दादी! मेला हम आपके पास ले आए!_
DADI (surprised, happy): Oh my! All of you, here? You left the mela?
  _हिंदी: अरे! तुम सब यहाँ? मेला छोड़कर?_

### Shot 5.2 — MEDIUM — Chhotu, Dadi
ACTION: Chhotu sits beside Dadi on the charpai and holds her hand.
CHHOTU (sincere): Sorry, Dadi. You called me this morning and I didn't come.
  _हिंदी: सॉरी दादी। सुबह आपने बुलाया था और मैं नहीं आया।_
DADI (warm): Oh, you silly boy… you're here now. That's more than enough.
  _हिंदी: अरे पगले… तू आ गया, यही बहुत है।_

### Shot 5.3 — WIDE — the whole village around the charpai
ACTION: Gudiya, Raju, Lallan, Masterji and Chacha sit on the ground around the charpai; more village children gather.
GUDIYA (eager): Dadi, tell us a story, please!
  _हिंदी: दादी, कहानी सुनाइए ना!_
DADI (smiling, remembering): Then listen… this is the story of my very first Diwali. Back then, this village didn't even have electricity…
  _हिंदी: तो सुनो… मेरी पहली दिवाली की कहानी। तब इस गाँव में बिजली भी नहीं थी…_
LALLAN (offering): And here you go, Dadi — my sweets. Today, with FULL sugar!
  _हिंदी: और ये लीजिए दादी — मेरी मिठाई। आज पूरी चीनी वाली!_
EVERYONE (laughing): Hahaha!
  _हिंदी: हाहाहा!_

### Shot 5.4 — CLOSE — Dadi
ACTION: Dadi looks around at everyone, eyes shining.
DADI (moved): Children, Diwali isn't lit up by diyas… it's lit up by sitting together with the people you love.
  _हिंदी: बच्चो, दिवाली दीयों से नहीं… अपनों के साथ बैठने से रोशन होती है।_

### Shot 5.5 — MEDIUM — Jugaadu Chacha, Sheru
ACTION: The switched-off radio suddenly crackles on by itself, pointing at Sheru.
RADIO (Sheru's heart, hopeful): So… do I get a laddoo now?
  _हिंदी: अब तो लड्डू मिलेगा?_
JUGAADU CHACHA (laughing): Hahaha! This one definitely gets a laddoo!
  _हिंदी: हाहाहा! इसका तो पक्का मिलेगा!_
[SHORT]

### Shot 5.6 — WIDE — everyone
ACTION: Hold on the warm courtyard full of people and diyas.
END CARD: Spend time with your elders — they need us just as much as we need them.
  _हिंदी: बड़ों के साथ समय बिताओ — उन्हें हमारी उतनी ही ज़रूरत है जितनी हमें उनकी।_
