# Kulfi Kahani — Jugaadu Chacha gadget story bank (draft 9 Oct 2026, for owner approval)

Formula (Motu Patlu idea, our own world): Jugaadu Chacha builds a gadget from village junk -> the kids use it for fun -> it does
TOO much and spreads chaos through the village (walks, chases, sneaks, takes) -> someone gets hurt or sad (real emotion) -> the kids
fix it with their own goodness/cleverness, not the gadget -> moral, said simply. The gadget must ACT on the story, never decorate it.
