# Free 3D assets for an Indian village (monetised YouTube kids' cartoons, Blender)

Researched 2 Oct 2026. Web research only: nothing downloaded, no accounts made.

**Licences accepted:** CC0, CC-BY (with credit), MIT/Apache, Sketchfab "Free Standard".
**Licences rejected:** NC, ND, SA (see the note below), editorial, personal-use, and anything unclear.

How each licence was checked:
- Sketchfab: read per model from the public API (`api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/<uid>` and `/v3/search`) on 2 Oct 2026. The `license.label` field is quoted exactly.
- Quaternius, Kenney and BlenderKit: read from their own pack or licence pages.
- **Before any download, check the licence again on the model page.** Authors can change a licence for future downloads. Save a screenshot or PDF of the page next to the file in `assets/` as proof.

### Licence notes
- **CC-BY (4.0 on Sketchfab):** monetised YouTube use is fine. You must credit the author, the title, a link and the licence, for example in the video description. Changes are allowed, but say "modified". Keep `CREDITS.md` in the repo and paste it into every description.
- **Sketchfab "Free Standard":** since 2024–25 Sketchfab lets authors give models away free under its Standard License. Sketchfab describes that licence as worldwide, all media, commercial and non-commercial, derivatives allowed, and "No credit needs to be given". Some models also carry a "NoAI" tag, which only bans use in AI training or generation. **Caveat:** sketchfab.com/licenses blocked my fetcher, so this is based on Sketchfab's own search snippet rather than the full text. Read the full Standard License once before relying on it. Until then these items are listed as *conditional*.
- **CC BY-SA:** allowed commercially, but ShareAlike could arguably pull your derived model files under SA. It is left out to keep things simple.
- **NoAI tag:** many Sketchfab CC-BY models say "NoAI". That is fine for rendering cartoons. It does matter if we ever feed the models into an AI image or video generator, so don't.
- **Trade dress:** the auto-rickshaw and the Hindustan Ambassador look like real products (Bajaj, HM). Remove badges and logos. Generic shapes are fine.
- **AI-made or ripped uploads:** some 2025–26 Sketchfab uploads with 400k–800k faces look AI-generated (Meshy/Tripo-style). Some, like "for Hindi Kahaniya", may copy other channels' characters. The uploader cannot license something they don't own, so these are marked **unverified provenance** and excluded.

## 3) Style-match notes (so the village looks like one world)

1. **Backbone: Quaternius plus Kenney, all CC0.** Both use flat vertex or material colours with no textures, chunky low-poly shapes and similar bevel-free silhouettes. They mix well. Quaternius animals and characters are rigged and animated, which matters most for cartoons. Use them for the trees, palms, rocks, grass, fences, farm animals, villagers' bodies and props.
2. **Indian buildings: the nayan pachori set (CC-BY)** is the one coherent Indian village set: houses, hut, well, handpump, water tank, panchayat, shop, charpai and railway track, all 250–2,000 faces. These use **image textures**, not flat colours. To match the Quaternius/Kenney look, either:
   - (a) bake or average each texture into 3–6 flat material colours, or
   - (b) put the whole scene through one toon shader (Shader-to-RGB ramp plus Freestyle/outline) so the textures read as painted.

   Option (b) is the cheaper way to unify everything.
3. **nitin0illigel (Desi Hut, Tulsi) and rmourya (Temple Low poly)** are flat-colour LP-toon. They are the closest match to Quaternius with no rework.
4. **rSquare** (auto-rickshaw, Ambassador, South Indian temple kit) is clean mid-low-poly with simple textures. It works next to the nayan set.
5. **Avoid mixing in** realistic or scanned items (Rahul Barate matkas, the tiffin scan, the 93k bullock cart, the cycle rickshaw scan, the Sufyan charpai scan). Decimate heavily and recolour them flat, or remodel. A 4K PBR pot next to a 300-face toon hut looks wrong.
6. **Scale:** Quaternius is roughly 1 unit = 1 m. Sketchfab uploads vary a lot. Normalise everything against a 1.7 m villager.
7. **One palette:** pick ~16 village colours (terracotta, lime-white, indigo-blue door, turmeric yellow, neem green, laterite red, mud brown, sky). Recolour every imported material to that palette in a Blender script. This does more for consistency than any choice of pack.

### Download checklist when we pull assets (later)
1. Open each page, confirm the licence label still matches this file, and save the page as PDF into `assets/<name>/LICENSE_PROOF.pdf`.
2. Add a line to `CREDITS.md`: Title, Author, URL, Licence, "modified" yes/no.
3. Paste the CC-BY credits into every YouTube description that shows those assets.
4. Do not redistribute the raw model files publicly. The toon-render-lab repo is **public**, so keep downloaded third-party meshes out of git (`.gitignore` `assets/thirdparty/`), or keep only CC0 ones in it.
